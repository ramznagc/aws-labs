# 📦 Amazon S3 Lifecycle Management

## What is Lifecycle Management?

Lifecycle Management automatically transitions or deletes objects based on predefined rules.

---

## Benefits

- Reduce storage costs
- Automate storage optimization
- Archive unused data
- Remove expired objects

---

## Lifecycle Actions

- Transition to Standard-IA
- Transition to One Zone-IA
- Transition to Glacier
- Expire Objects

---

## Best Practices

- Apply lifecycle rules to appropriate prefixes.
- Use lifecycle transitions for infrequently accessed data.
- Review lifecycle policies regularly.

---

## Key Takeaways

Lifecycle policies automate storage management while optimizing cost and operational efficiency.
